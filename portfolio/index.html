Case Study

Designing a mobile portfolio management experience

Bloomberg Professional App (Apple) (Google Play)

Results
  • Usage increased by 20%, PORT adoption increased by 15%
  • User satisfaction increased
  • Global wide positive impact for UX buy-in from comprehensive research findings. Leveraged by other teams and projects.
Team
  • Senior Interaction Designer & User Researcher (Annabel Ruddle)
  • Product Manager - Mobile
  • Product Owner - PORT
  • Terminal Port Team
  • Mobile Engineering
  • Terminal Engineering
Project Duration

4 months

Platform

IOS & Android

Tools
  • Figma
  • Google Docs
  • Google Sheets

Problem

Currently on PORT* on the Bloomberg Professional App, it is not easy to get a quick summary of how a portfolio is doing, this combined with the lack of feature parity with the Bloomberg Terminal, has lead to consistent low usage.

*PORT is a portfolio management product on the Bloomberg Professional App

Opportunity

Increase value and decrease friction of information gathering by creating a summary view on launch of PORT on the Bloomberg Professional App and increasing user value will lead to increased overall PORT usage.

Definition to Ideation

To get to the solution I did a bunch of UX activities such as conducting stakeholder interviews and gathering current user data to write a UX brief that included a project timeline from ideation to research to build, technical considerations and limitations, usecases, and user stories. After I created personas, conducted a current state audit as well as competitive analysis, mapped out the ideal userflow for our hypothesis, then preceded to rapidly sketch ideas on paper and then take the best ones and design them out into concept boards and concept prototypes in Figma to show my stakeholders.

User Research

Methodology

  • Background & Workflow Questions
  • Card Sorting Activity
  • Concept Evaluation
  • Remote Sessions over Zoom and Microsoft Teams
  • 60 minutes

Participants

  • 15 Portfolio Managers across asset classes and domains

Key Questions

  • What data do clients want to see? What data do clients not want to see?
  • How / when / where are clients using PORT currently?
  • What is critical to build now and what can we build later?
  • What factors determine adoption and increase usage?

Access Points & Entrances

Three different entrances were tested with the intention of making it easier to discover and access this new PORT experience with local and global access points: 1. Home Promo Card 2. Search by portfolio name 3. PORT grid card 4. My Portfolios. Home Promo Card performed the best followed by PORT grid card.

Iteration

Based on the research findings I gathered the team and we ideated and discussed what iterations we wanted to move forward with. Including but not all:

  • Have dedicated column sets for each main asset class, increasing cross-platform consistency and ensuring data shows up the way users expect and want in Holdings
  • Make entrance to holdings view bigger on Summary Overview by including a button bar with the first button as Holdings
  • Consolidate and move risk metrics, ESG, and other secondary metrics to end of feed
  • Summary cards link to Detail pages where users can go deeper into the data

I iterated the design, added interaction guidelines and documentation and worked with engineering through build, QA, to launch.

Results

The new mobile PORT experience was rolled out internally first and then to clients to great success resulting in a huge jump in both usage and adoption. Most notably re-capturing and re-engaging one of the biggest user types, the Fixed Income portfolio managers. Research insights were so successful that they were leveraged by parallel product and engineering teams.

  • Increase in usage
  • Increase in adoption
  • Increase in user satisfaction
