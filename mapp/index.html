Case Study

Designing a mobile app for Misfits Market

View on App Store — Misfits Market

Results: Successful app launch, Featured in the App Store

Team: Product Designer (Sole), Software Engineer, Project Manager

Duration: 6 months

Tools: Figma, Whimsical

Opportunity

Misfits Market wanted a mobile app of the current online experience

  • It serves the user by creating an easy to reach / always accessible Misfits Market experience where they can shop, view current order, manage account, and more.
  • It serves the business by getting key phone space (therefore users’ attention), encouraging being top of mind. It also adds legitimacy to the brand and stabilizes investment from current investors who like to see material progress.

www.misfitsmarket.com — Misfits Market Mobile App

System Design

I designed the atoms first, then the main components, then the elements, and then the screens. I made it easy for development by re-using components as much as possible and by utilizing a ‘friendlier’ font SF Pro instead of Sharp Grotesk to ensure accessibility and usability across all types of devices.
