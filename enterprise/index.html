Business Managers keep track of royalty earnings in a “Client Tracker”

Trade-offs

1. Recoupment Status: tech not there —> so showed Activity date & Statement date separately

2. Normalizing Royalties earned in multiple currencies: Tech not there & increase scope, need to store historical exchange rate data. Increase margin of error & liability —> display royalty earned in each currency separately

3. Support for multiple types of data vis: increase scope —> 1 main data vis: bar chart

4. Self setup: Increase scope, many unknowns, Hifi wanted complete control over who signed up for the beta —> white glove service

5. Custom dashboards: Increase scope, tech not there, too complex to do within deadline —> Client Dashboards only with a searchable list of all clients as home

6. Notifications: Increase scope, secondary functionality —> scope in for a later version

  • The user flows were made in Sketch, and for wireframing & prototyping I moved to Figma as it has more robust prototyping and collaboration capabilities.
  • The flows and sitemaps helped to align the team and get stakeholder sign off to move to the next phase: designing the prototype for testing.
  • I utilized a lot of user research and some competitive research to guide form factor, narrow focus, and inform my design choices.
  • Since testing would be done with real business managers who were not use to seeing wireframes, I decided a rough level of fidelity would be distracting and designed the MVP prototype in high fidelity with lots of interaction. It looked and felt real and got feedback exactly where it was desired: on the core features.
