About

Case Study

Building a UX framework for user education for Bloomberg

Bloomberg Professional App (Apple) (Google Play)

Results
  • Clear documentation
  • Systemized use of education patterns across 45 teams
  • Increased total usage of app by 15%
Team
  • Annabel - Senior Interaction Designer (Lead Designer)
  • Product Manager
  • Engineers
Project Duration

5 months

Platform

IOS & Android

Tools
  • Figma
  • Google Docs
  • Google Sheets

Problem

Bloomberg Anywhere* users were not using the app to its fullest potential.

*Bloomberg Anywhere = Bloomberg Terminal + Bloomberg Professional App

Opportunity

“How-To” a centralized self-discovery resource

Current State

Ideate & Narrow

Explored a number of approaches and ideas, using the existing tour component and narrowed down to two…

Approach 1

Collection First List Approach

  • Content is organized into Collections
  • “Apple Tips”

Approach 2

Content First Feed Approach

  • No hierarchy, no layers to go through, just straight into the content
  • “Tiktok, IG”

User Research

Tested 2 approaches/variants (A&B) of this resource with 11 clients, in-person and remote to understand which was more valuable, current client modes and methods of learning (answering the how, when, & where), and to evaluate the effectiveness of our hypothesis to increase overall engagement and feature adoption.
